# Decision Packet Template

Keep `DECISION_PACKET.md` compact enough for Opus or Fable to navigate without
reading every full report.

```markdown
# Decision Packet: <problem>

## Destination

## Executive state
- Cycle:
- Coverage status: sufficient | insufficient | contradictory
- Overall confidence:
- Decision currently blocked by:

## Original claims
| Claim | Source | Evidence status | Current verdict |
|---|---|---|---|

## Production reality

## What prior work solved

## What prior work partially solved

## What remains unsolved

## Material contradictions
| Contradiction | Evidence on each side | Likely explanation | Discriminating probe | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Gap ledger
| Gap | Type | Impact | Evidence | Blocking? |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Candidate problem framings
| Framing | Evidence for | Evidence against | Consequence if chosen |
|---|---|---|---|

## Open decisions
| Decision | Why it matters | Options | Evidence needed | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Suggested next probes

## Source pointers

## Full reports
```

Do not include a final implementation recommendation. The Opus navigator or
explicit Fable escalation owns that judgment.
